From the ALX Data Analytics Program

Maji Ndogo water-access analytics — a 4-part SQL series

MySQL · SQL joins, CTEs & views · ERD design · Data cleaning

A four-part case study analyzing a 60,000-record water-access database for the fictional Maji Ndogo region. Started with exploratory SQL and data cleaning, moved through clustering and queue-time analysis, audited data integrity against independent auditor reports, and finished with a full cross-table synthesis and improvement plan.

  • Shared taps serve 43% of the population, with average queue times of 123 minutes
  • Only 28% of tested wells provide clean water, and 45% of in-home taps are non-functional
  • Cross-checked employee-recorded water quality scores against independent audits — found a 6% discrepancy rate and isolated four employees with unusually high error rates
